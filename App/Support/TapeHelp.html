Tape Help

Record, cut and mix audio without fuss. Hover over any control to see what it does.

Getting started

Drop audio files on the window or choose File > Import Audio (Command I). Each file becomes a region on the timeline. The source files are never changed: every edit only changes which part of a file plays, where and how loud.

To record, pick the input in the record bar at the top of the window and press R. R or Space stops. The new take lands at the playhead on the selected track.

Zoom with Command = and Command -, Option scroll, or the overview bar at the top. Shift Z fits everything.

Editing

Every region has a name strip at the top and its waveform below.

Drag the name stripMoves the region. Option drag moves a copy. In multitrack view, drag up or down to move it to another track.
Drag in the waveformMarks a range of audio. Shift drag marks a range anywhere.
Drag a region edgeTrims the region.
Fade handlesDrag the small handles at the top corners of the waveform to fade in or out. Option drag the midpoint to change the shape. Shift drag fades all selected regions.
BladeCommand B splits the selected regions at the playhead, or at both range edges. B picks the blade tool, A the select tool.
Nudge, and . move the selected regions by the nudge step set in the Inspector.
SnapEdits snap to zero crossings (N toggles). Hold Command while dragging to switch snapping off.

Every edit can be undone with Command Z.

Cutting out

Mark the part you do not want by dragging in the waveform, then press Delete or click Cut Out in the transport bar. The marked audio is removed and everything after it on that track moves left to close the gap.

Forward Delete removes the marked audio but leaves the gap, so nothing moves.

With nothing marked, Delete removes the selected regions.

Split. The Split button next to Cut Out splits the regions at the playhead (click in a waveform to place it), or at both edges of the marked range, and removes nothing. It does the same as Command B. The parts can then be moved, trimmed, faded or deleted on their own.

Crossfade cuts. When this is on (Edit > Crossfade Cuts, or the Editing section of the Inspector), the two parts that meet at a cut overlap by a short crossfade, so the cut does not click. The length is set next to the switch; 10 ms is a good start for speech. When the gap stays, both cut edges get a short fade instead.

Playback and loop

Space plays and pauses. J, K and L shuttle backwards, stop and forwards; press J or L again to go faster.

I and O set the range start and end at the playhead. Option X clears the range.

The loop button in the transport bar (Command L) repeats the marked range. Without a range it loops the selected regions, and without a selection the whole project.

Tracks

Shift Command M switches between the single track view and the multitrack view. Command T adds a track.

Each track header has, from left to right: the color swatch, the name (double click to rename, drag to reorder), the magnet, Mute (M), Solo (S), a gain slider, a pan slider and a level meter. Double click the gain slider for 0 dB, or the pan slider to center it. Click a meter to clear its clip light.

The master strip at the top controls the level of the whole mix.

Magnetic tracks

Click the magnet in a track header (Shift Command G) to make the track magnetic. Its regions then follow each other without gaps, starting where the first region was. Dragging a region reorders the sequence; an insertion line shows where it lands. Removing a region closes the gap, unless you use Forward Delete.

A region on another track can be connected to a region on a magnetic track: Option Command drag it, or use Connect to in the Inspector. Connected regions move along with their anchor.

Process and export

The Process menu applies Normalize, Gain, Remove DC Offset, Trim Silence, Reverse, Compressor, Limiter and Resample to the selected regions. Most of them can be previewed before you apply.

File > Export (Command E) writes the mix or the range to WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, ALAC or AAC. Export Each Region (Shift Command E) writes every selected region to its own file.
